## Confirmed request
Replace the old outline and visual arrangement. Design freedom delegated. First-time audience. Quality, clarity, responsive controls, purposeful video. Preserve the old deck.
## Approach
12 main slides and 2 appendix slides. Show the promise, the gap after translation, the writing-learning-accumulation loop, and actual video before the PO argument.
## Visual world
Inherit Say My Day's illustrated diary and ink line bird. Paper white, warm neutral paper, near-black cinema for the demo and prompt narrative. Locally hosted Pretendard; actual wordmark. Large natural Korean headlines. No repeating numbered kickers or card grids.

A diary sentence is the through-line. Three simple journey selectors, scene-linked video playback, before/after comparison, prompt revision timeline, an incident case browser. Detailed source text stays in a quiet appendix.
