## Stack

## Users
People seeing Say My Day for the first time, including reviewers evaluating the creator's PO decisions and AI-assisted process.
## Product Purpose
Explain a diary-based language learning app, demonstrate its actual use, and make product choices and evidence understandable.
## Positioning
The user's own diary becomes translated sentences, pronunciation guides, words, grammar, listening material and review material.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Korean to Chinese, Korean to Japanese, Chinese to Japanese. Local diary storage; text sent for translation. Cumulative learning days and bird growth. Microphone is off by default. User testing and learning effectiveness are not established.
## Brand Commitments
Say My Day wordmark; small bird holding a diary, pen by its head; illustrated diary identity. The user authorized layout freedom and adapting the app concept.
## Evidence on Hand
../images; ../metrics.json; ../프롬프트.md; ../stability.md; existing edited demo. No invented test outcomes, measured gains or development incidents.
## Product Principles
Explain the product before explaining implementation. Show actual use before analysis. Make PO choices explicit. Keep primary slides simple and reserve complete records for the appendix.
