# Internal quality bar — Say My Day presentation
This is an authored internal calibration for an app-derived presentation. No external catalog quality board was supplied by the degraded concept-seed run. It does not claim an independent competitor benchmark.

## Audience clarity
Before technical detail, the visitor can explain what the app turns into learning material, what the learner does, and what is retained.
## Editorial craft
Distinct compositions for promise, problem, demonstration, decisions, measurements, process and close. Large natural Korean text; no forced question-answer headings on every page.
## Brand discipline
Keep the existing notebook-carrying bird and wordmark. Paper and ink carry the deck. Illustrative diary text is a narrative example, never a user testimonial.
## Interaction discipline
A control exists to inspect a meaningful alternative, a real scene or a source. Important meaning remains visible without opening every detail. Video is user-triggered, with native controls and clearly named scene choices.
## Mobile floor
Korean words do not split into stray syllables. Tabs are all visible or deliberately wrap. Text is readable at native mobile size, with intentional vertical scrolling. Code and long paths may wrap independently.
## Evidence discipline
Balanced screen metrics retain scope and caveats. No claimed user-test outcome or measured learning/retention gain. Actual incident records and original prompt changes remain inspectable in the appendix.
